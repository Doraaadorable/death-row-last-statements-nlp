# Claim interpretation: development addendum (draft v1)

Date: 2026-11-06. Written after discussing the fixed 21-document sample.
This is a post-observation proposal, not a preregistration, frozen guideline,
owner-adjudicated dataset, or independent validation.

## Scope and unit
Select propositions with complete prepared context. The 27 rows in
reports/semantic_development_proposals_v1.csv are illustrative selections from
18 documents, not exhaustive annotations. The coverage table retains all 21,
including three with no selected proposition. Coverage means discussed, not
owner-confirmed. No original annotations, sample or retrieval rules change.

The focused target remains an explicit personal denial of performing a killing.
Knowledge, reported views, hypothetical acts and unspecified responsibility are
contrast cases. Do not silently broaden the target to every kind of blame,
crime, intent or judicial criticism.

## Four existing fields
Source is whose selected claim/view is presented, not merely who utters the
whole statement. Actor is the participant who performs the selected act or
holds the selected knowledge. Other is relative to the speaker.
Event type killing_action requires language specifying taking a life; shot,
crime, blame and responsible do not individually establish that event.
Use other for a resolved non-killing type, unclear for an unresolved event.
Polarity concerns the selected predicate, not a nearby predicate or actual truth.

## Auxiliary fields proposed after discussion
statement_mode preserves assertion, attributed_view, reported_confession,
embedded_request, embedded_conditional, hypothetical or imperative.
These are practical development descriptions, not an exhaustive mutually
exclusive linguistic taxonomy. One value records the most relevant boundary.
event_reference records the event referent and remaining ambiguity.

A hypothetical killing or imperative can have positive surface wording without
asserting an actual event. Their proposed polarity is unclear under the current
four-field scheme; the mode and note must be read with it. This temporary
representation needs owner review before any evaluation.
In an embedded forgiveness request, affirmed records positive self-attribution
within the request, not a standalone factual assertion.

## Boundaries to apply consistently
- Do not transfer negation of knowledge, understanding, forgiveness or speech to killing.
- Keep an embedded belief holder separate from the matrix knowledge holder.
- Separate quoted/reported, attributed, and anticipated views from verified speech.
- Resolve this/it only from supplied context; absence of context stays unresolved.
- Preserve passive agents: lives were not taken by me explicitly denies own agency.
- Innocence status, denial of a specified action and judicial criticism are distinct.
- Responsibility and harmful decisions need not specify killing.
- Do not infer actual guilt, sincerity, motive or psychological acceptance.
- Keep transcription anomalies; do not silently repair them into evidence.

## Retrieval observations, not performance estimates
Execution 231 is an observed nonmatch containing a passive personal killing
denial. Execution 60 contains case responsibility without a specified act.
Murderer and taking his life reveal lexical omissions in documents retrieved
by other cues. These observations do not estimate recall from six nonmatches.
Freeze screen v1 during this review; a later retrieval revision needs its own
version and comparison against unchanged inputs.
