## Execution 451
A useful knowledge span is I don't know who you are. Its expressed source
and knowledge holder are the speaker; the negated proposition is knowing the
addressee's identity. The addressee is not the holder of that knowledge.

I hurt a lot of people with decisions I made explicitly attributes harm to
the speaker. Do not infer the type of physical act or identify every affected
person from this alone. The source's This person shoe did that wording is
retained as a transcription boundary; no silent correction supplies evidence.

## Execution 471
I don't know if you believe me has at least two distinguishable layers:
the speaker denies knowing, while the embedded belief holder is you.
The negation does not assert that the listener disbelieves, and the word
believe alone does not show a claim being attributed to another person.

I take the full blame for this expresses blame acceptance, but the specific
event referred to by this is not made explicit in the supplied statement.
I have accepted everything is also too broad to label acceptance of a
particular conviction, sentence or judicial rationale. The words forgive,
victim's family and blame do not independently resolve the act as a killing.

## Execution 297
The statement criticizes representation, procedural safeguards, clemency and
the death penalty. This record analyzes those as the speaker's expressed
positions; it does not verify the legal or factual claims.
Generic when-an-attorney constructions are not automatically specific
verified events in this speaker's case.

The statements about innocent people do not alone establish explicit self-
denial. Context can suggest personal relevance without satisfying a narrow
explicit-self-denial target. The request for the family to find forgiveness
also does not specify a denied or admitted act.

## Cross-document guideline candidates, not frozen revisions
- Review a selected proposition with complete context, not an entire person.
- Negation of knowing, understanding, identity knowledge or speech planning
  does not transfer to a separate action proposition.
- Separate the matrix knowledge holder from an embedded action or belief
  participant.
- Preserve whether harm attribution is direct, or embedded in an apology or
  forgiveness request; do not equate all these forms.
- Resolve it/this only with adequate supplied context. Unresolved event
  identity does not imply uncertain polarity of a different, clear proposition.
- Separate general institutional criticism from explicit personal denial.
- Do not infer private psychological acceptance, sincerity or actual guilt.
