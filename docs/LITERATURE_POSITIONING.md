# Literature positioning: initial search
Date: 2026-11-06

## Scope
This is a targeted initial search, not a systematic review or proof of novelty.
Queries combined death-row last statements with thematic/text analysis, NLP,
responsibility, innocence, negation scope, attribution and behavioral testing.
Publisher pages and ACL Anthology records were consulted. The access level
below distinguishes full-text sections from abstracts. No model benchmark was
run during this literature search.

## Relevant prior work

| Work | Evidence consulted | Relevance and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Malaga and Delgado (2020), Death row last statements: a phenomenological analysis | Publisher abstract indexed in search; direct full-page fetch returned 403 | Reports thematic work on 61 Texas statements, including affection, religion, regret and gratitude. Rediscovering these themes is not a new research claim. |
| Mehra et al. (2024), Consistency in verbal expression of death row criminals: concluding life with emotions | Publisher full-text methods sections and abstract | Analyzes 466 statements using computational techniques, including BERT embeddings and emotion analysis. Using BERT on this corpus is not by itself novel. We have not independently audited their pipeline or psychological interpretations. |
| Ostman and Halmari (2025), Death-Row inmates' last statements: Establishing identity through expressions of responsibility | Publisher abstract and outline only | Responsibility and its linguistic expression are already studied in this genre. Do not claim this project introduces that topic. |
| Pareti (2016), PARC 3.0: A Corpus of Attribution Relations | ACL Anthology abstract | Treats attribution through source, cue and content. This motivates separating who makes a claim from the person mentioned inside it; a news attribution corpus is not validated gold data for last statements. |
| Fancellu et al. (2017), Detecting negation scope is easy, except when it isn't | ACL Anthology abstract | Shows why apparent negation success can depend on annotation conventions and easy cases. Retaining not in TF-IDF is not an evaluation of scope understanding. |
| Ribeiro et al. (2020), Beyond Accuracy: Behavioral Testing of NLP Models with CheckList | ACL Anthology abstract | Motivates capability-focused diagnostic examples. A small targeted challenge set is not a representative accuracy estimate. |

## Positioning decision
The current NMF project contributes a documented exploratory analysis and
diagnostics; its themes are not presented as new discoveries.
A possible extension is an evidence-backed, domain-specific audit of claim
attribution and negation target. This is a candidate contribution, not a claim
that no similar benchmark exists.

Before publication claims, read the closest works in full, follow their
references and citations, and search explicitly for last-statement claim
attribution, denial/admission and challenge-set evaluation. This limited
search cannot establish a research gap or publication readiness.
