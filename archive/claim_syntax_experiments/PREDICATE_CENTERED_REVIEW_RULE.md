# Predicate-centered review rule

Date: 2026-11-07 (America/Toronto).

## Owner-approved principle
The owner explicitly requested objective selection criteria, avoiding selection
of whichever verb seems more important. Identify the central predicate for
each proposition record, then analyze that proposition in full context.
This is a review principle, not evidence that all earlier selections were
exhaustive or independently annotated.

## Operational procedure for the next annotation version
1. List predicate candidates in textual order within the review unit before
   assigning semantic outcomes. Include matrix and embedded predicates.
2. Treat auxiliaries, tense and modal support with their lexical predicate:
   did not kill centers on kill, not did as an additional action.
   Copular predicates include their complement (was responsible); conventional
   responsibility expressions can be multiword (take the full blame).
   Record uncertain predicate boundaries rather than forcing agreement.
3. For each candidate, apply a written task/contrast inclusion criterion and
   record the reason for inclusion or exclusion. Do not rank importance,
   vividness or usefulness for a desired result.
4. For every included proposition record, identify one predicate focus by
   exact source offsets. Distinct eligible predicates may yield distinct rows
   from the same sentence. Identifying a focus does not authorize selecting
   every predicate regardless of research scope.
5. Keep governing negation, modality, reporting frames, arguments and reference
   context. Focus offsets supplement, not replace, the evidence span and full
   statement. Assign source, event type, actor, polarity and proposition status
   relative to that selected focus.
6. Keep unresolved references or disputed boundaries explicit and version any
   revision. Do not infer actual guilt, intention or sincerity.

## Examples
I do not understand why I did it:
- understand: matrix knowledge/understanding proposition, negated;
- did: embedded action proposition; action identity remains unresolved.
These are distinct records if both satisfy the declared inclusion criterion.

I know you think I did this:
- know, think and did are predicate candidates in order.
- If did is selected, you think and I know remain context for attribution.
- Selecting did does not mean it is more important, and does not make it a
  self-owned admission. Other candidates must be considered under the same
  inclusion criteria, not silently ignored.

## Preservation and evaluation
The four focus suggestions in METHODS.md are
illustrations, not an exhaustive predicate inventory. This approval does not
retroactively add focus offsets or new owner-confirmed predicate rows to the
existing 37 selections. Those selections and diagnostic results stay intact.

This rule reduces discretionary choices but does not eliminate interpretive
judgment. A pilot inventory and explicit inclusion/exclusion decisions are
needed before claiming reproducible or complete predicate selection.
If human-supplied focus is given to a computational method, disclose the extra
oracle input, provide comparable inputs to baselines, or evaluate automatic
focus identification separately. No diagnostic results are changed here.
